400m PROBE ARM: encoder_lr_scale 01 and 8 epochs, at lr 2e-4 and batch 12, both confirmed winners at every scale. These are the two axes never swept above 50m -- the size grids used only encoder_lr_scale 0.5 and 1.0 and pinned epochs at 4, while the 216-arm 50m grid also covered 0 and 0.1. A larger encoder holds more pretrained structure to lose, so gentler updates are where a 400m would plausibly differ from a 200m; and a 400m may want fewer or more passes than the 4 inherited from smaller models. lr is not a candidate: the 400m optimum is 2e-4, the middle of the range, with both neighbours worse.
