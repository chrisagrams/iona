The 400m denoise fine-tune under DeepSpeed ZeRO-2, and the template for the 400m grid. Identical to the 50m DeepSpeed config except for the checkpoint, so any difference between the two size ladders is attributable to encoder capacity alone. The 50m grid's 216 arms decided which axes this grid still needs to sweep. SEED REPETITION (FT5): the 400m encoder at the configuration both finished grids selected -- lr 2e-4, encoder at 0.5x the head's rate, 4 epochs, head width 512, effective batch 12 -- repeated at seed 2 of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. Only the seed varies, and it moves head initialisation, data order and dropout; the train/validation/test split comes from the dataset itself and is identical across every arm, so these six numbers measure training noise alone. Their spread is the resolution of the 216-arm 50m grid and the 12-arm 100m grid, whose top clusters span 0.0023 and 0.0012 test AUROC respectively. It also decides whether 100m's 0.9403 genuinely beats 50m's 0.9320, a gap of 0.0083 that currently rests on one sample each.
