The 50m denoise baseline under DeepSpeed ZeRO-2 on all twelve tiles of a node. ZeRO-2 replaces the DDP reducer, which is what faults, and is 21.4x the throughput of one tile. This is the grid template. Effective batch is 48 rather than 4, so warmup is 42 steps rather than 500 and freeze_encoder_steps is 0. SEED REPETITION (FT5): the 50m encoder at the configuration both finished grids selected -- lr 2e-4, encoder at 0.5x the head's rate, 4 epochs, head width 512, effective batch 12 -- repeated at seed 6 of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. Only the seed varies, and it moves head initialisation, data order and dropout; the train/validation/test split comes from the dataset itself and is identical across every arm, so these six numbers measure training noise alone. Their spread is the resolution of the 216-arm 50m grid and the 12-arm 100m grid, whose top clusters span 0.0023 and 0.0012 test AUROC respectively. It also decides whether 100m's 0.9403 genuinely beats 50m's 0.9320, a gap of 0.0083 that currently rests on one sample each.
