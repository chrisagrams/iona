The 50m denoise baseline under DeepSpeed ZeRO-2 on all twelve tiles of a node. ZeRO-2 replaces the DDP reducer, which is what faults, and is 21.4x the throughput of one tile. This is the grid template. Effective batch is 48 rather than 4, so warmup is 42 steps rather than 500 and freeze_encoder_steps is 0. GRID ARM: lr=1e-5, encoder learning at 1.0x the head's rate, 4 epochs, head width 256, effective batch 48 (4 per tile x 12 tiles x 1 accumulation) giving about 7,266 optimizer updates. The batch axis exists to test whether more, smaller updates beat fewer, larger ones; batch and update count move together, so it cannot separate the two.
